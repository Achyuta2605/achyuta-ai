Companies planned to roughly double their AI spend this year. On average they spent **4.7x** last year's amount ([Menlo Ventures, 2026](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2026-the-state-of-enterprise-ai/)).

The reason is agents. A chatbot answers once. An agent plans, calls tools, checks its work and loops, and on every step it resends the whole context: system prompt, tool definitions and the conversation so far. Anthropic found agents use about **4x** the tokens of a chat, and multi-agent systems about **15x** ([Anthropic Engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system)).

## 1. Price the task, not the token

Measure what one finished job costs, from trace to total. Only **19%** of companies monitor AI costs continuously ([Cloudera survey via ITPro](https://www.itpro.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/ais-price-tag-is-starting-to-shape-its-path-through-the-enterprise-89-percent-of-enterprises-have-scaled-back-ai-deployments-over-token-costs-and-theyre-still-struggling-to-track-spending)).

## 2. Route by difficulty

LangChain's Open SWE coding agent picks a model tier for every task. Median cost per task fell from **$2.61 to $0.94** with no drop in merged PRs ([LangChain](https://www.langchain.com/blog/how-to-build-a-model-router-in-the-harness)).

## 3. Cache the repeated prefix

Every request resends the same system prompt and tool definitions. Prompt caching stores that prefix so it is billed at a fraction of the price. Notion uses it in Notion AI and cut costs by up to **90%** ([Claude customer story](https://claude.com/customers/notion)).

## 4. Fine-tune a small specialist

Checkr replaced GPT-4 with a small open model trained on its own background-check data: about 5x cheaper, much faster and more accurate ([Predibase](https://predibase.com/blog/how-checkr-streamlines-background-checks-with-fine-tuned-small-language)).

## Bonus: batch what is not urgent

Batch APIs from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google cost **50%** less.

Big models are not the problem. Using them for everything is.
